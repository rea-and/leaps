Privacy policy

Last updated August 14, 2026.

What Leaps stores

Leaps stores the goals, progress samples, imported data, and WHOOP data needed to show personal progress trends. This information is stored in the tracker's private SQLite database.

WHOOP connection

When you connect WHOOP, Leaps requests access to sleep, recovery, and cycle data. OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept only on the server running this tracker and are used solely to refresh the displayed progress data.

Sharing and retention

Leaps does not sell, rent, or share this personal data with third parties. Data remains stored until the tracker owner deletes the database or removes the connection.

Contact

For questions about this tracker, contact its owner through the site hosting Leaps.
